Reducing unnecessary C-sections

In Egypt the C-section rate rose from 27.6% in 2008 to 51.8% in 2014 and 72.2% in 2021. Birth is a medical event and also a moment of autonomy and dignity: informed consent, power dynamics and respectful care. This matters to me because choice in childbirth should be informed, not fear-driven.

72.2%C-section rate in 2021, up from 51.8% in 2014 and 27.6% in 2008.
Myth: a C-section is the easier way.

Truth: it saves lives when needed, and it is still major surgery.

Four parts to the plan

Medical students workshop, antenatal classes, online awareness, community outreach.
